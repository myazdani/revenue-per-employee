What the chart measures

Revenue per employee = revenue ÷ headcount. Move right to see more employees; move up to see more revenue per employee. Equal horizontal distances represent equal multiples of headcount. Filled circle area uses one revenue scale across every year and filter.

Annual revenue is paired with a point-in-time workforce count. Late-year cuts can raise the ratio mechanically. Contractor labor, outsourcing, compute spending, profitability, and product quality are not captured.

What is reported, estimated, or projected?

The original 400 observations preserve the CSV’s exact Status field. “Source-reported” means the author labels a row Actual; it does not mean this reproduction has independently audited it. Mixed Actual/Estimate rows are classified as estimates. The original 2026 dataset contains 13 source-reported rows, 2 estimates, and 36 projections. Added media observations link directly to their source filings.

The dataset mixes fiscal-year and calendar-year labels, private-company estimates, run rates, and some currency-converted revenues. Compare business models with care: product resale, marketplace transactions, software subscriptions, and AI services recognize revenue differently.

Streaming and media: periods, company scope, and estimates

Disney, Comcast, Fox, and Roku use consolidated company figures. These are not standalone Disney+, Peacock, Tubi, or Roku Channel ratios. Netflix, Spotify, and the existing Amazon, Apple, and Alphabet observations are also available through the Streaming & media filter. Tubi is included separately from its parent Fox; the rows are comparisons, not an additive market total.

Skydance (Paramount + WBD) uses FY2025 merger pro forma revenue of $66.132B and combined year-end 2025 employees of about 53,100, giving about $1.25M per employee. It appears in the 2026 view as a comparison for the merged group, not a reported 2026 result. No predecessor history is spliced into a continuous merged-company series.

Tubi uses the disclosed FY2025 revenue floor of $1.1B and a management-stated 2026 team of about 700, giving a rough $1.57M comparison. Its bubble uses the revenue floor. Dates and workforce definitions differ, so this is neither a filed annual RPE nor a strict bound on actual RPE.

Disney, Comcast, and Roku retain their FY2025 values in the 2026 view, visibly marked as carried forward. Comcast reports full-time equivalents (FTEs); Fox and Roku report full-time employees. Disney includes full-time, part-time, and seasonal employees. Comcast’s FY2025 scope includes Versant before its January 2026 separation. These definitions affect comparisons.

Why the screenshot and annual dataset differ

Annual source dataset preserves the downloaded CSV. For 2026 it gives OpenAI $25B revenue / 3,800 employees = $6.579M per employee, and Anthropic $30B / 5,000 = $6M. Both are labeled Estimate/run-rate.

Match reference annotations changes only the final chart view. It holds OpenAI at its 2025 estimate ($13B / 2,300 = $5.652M) and shows Anthropic’s separately reported September 2026 TTM RPE of over $6.6M at approximately 5,300 employees. Anthropic’s October 26, 2026 S-1 ↗

The hollow Anthropic marker sits at the disclosed lower bound, has no revenue-size meaning, and is not connected to the annual series. The AI-lab ranking values and original CSV downloads retain the source values.

Time, trails, and restructuring annotations

Playback interpolates revenue and headcount only between neighboring annual rows, then recomputes their ratio. Those transitions are visual, not additional observations. Carried values remain still; Skydance and Tubi appear only at the 2026 comparison view, without an invented trail. OpenAI, Anthropic, and the added Fox series begin in 2023.

The 80 original source annotations have year labels only. Some describe workforce reductions; others describe business changes. They are reproduced as context, not verified layoff counts or evidence of AI causation. Display cohorts are editorial groupings; Streaming & media also includes relevant companies from other cohorts. The source’s original Group remains visible in company details.
